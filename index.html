Featured projects

Logistics Capstone: Power BI Dashboards

Power BI | four-page dashboard report

A capstone project on a trucking and logistics business. I built four dashboard pages covering operations, customers and revenue, fleet and fuel, and drivers and safety, with slicers so users can filter by customer type, load type, date, and employment status. Click an image to enlarge it.

  • Logistics Operation Overview: fuel cost per mile, loads, load revenue, on-time delivery, revenue by destination state and by month.
  • Customer and Revenue Performance: load revenue, revenue per customer, top 10 customers, revenue by customer type and by freight type.
  • Fleet and Fuel Performance: fuel spend, maintenance cost by type, fuel spend by year, average MPG, truck utilization versus downtime.
  • Driver and Safety Performance: driver on-time rate, active drivers, safety incidents by type, on-time rate by month, MPG versus on-time rate.

Maven Toy Store: Sales, Product, Inventory and Store Performance Analysis

Power BI | four-page dashboard report

A retail analysis for an e-commerce toy company, built to answer practical business questions: which products sell the most, which stores earn the most revenue, how much stock is available, and which products are out of stock. Click an image to enlarge it.

  • Product Analysis: total units sold, profit, revenue, profit margin, top 10 products and categories.
  • Inventory Analysis: inventory capital, stock by category, lowest stock products, out-of-stock products by store, and estimated lost value.
  • Store Analysis: revenue by store and city, units sold, and monthly revenue trend with a year filter.
